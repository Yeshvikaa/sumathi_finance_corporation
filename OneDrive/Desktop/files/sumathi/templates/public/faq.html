Who is eligible to apply for a loan?
Any resident individual or small business owner with a valid identity proof and a stable source of income may apply. Our team evaluates each application individually.
What documents are required?
You will need a valid identity proof (Aadhaar / Driving Licence / Passport), a recent photograph, and proof of address. Additional documents such as salary slips may be requested based on the loan amount.
How quickly will my loan be approved?
Most applications are reviewed within 48 hours of complete document submission. You will be notified of the outcome through the portal and via phone.
What repayment options are available?
We offer daily, weekly, and monthly repayment schedules. The frequency is agreed upon during approval based on your income cycle.
Can I pay using UPI or bank transfer?
Yes. We accept cash, UPI, and bank transfers. Digital payments must be submitted as a claim through the portal or WhatsApp and will be verified by our team.
Is my personal information kept confidential?
Absolutely. Your information is used solely for loan processing and is never shared with third parties. Please read our Privacy Policy for full details.
How do I track my application status?
Register on our customer portal, submit your application, and you can view real-time status updates including reviewer notes at any time.
