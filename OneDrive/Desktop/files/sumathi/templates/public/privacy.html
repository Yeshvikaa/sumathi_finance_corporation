Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide directly when you apply for a loan, contact us, or use our customer portal. This may include your full name, mobile number, email address, residential address, identity document details, and financial information.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively for:

  • Processing your loan application and verifying your identity
  • Communicating with you about your application and repayments
  • Maintaining accurate financial records as required by law
  • Sending payment reminders (via phone/WhatsApp)

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third party. Your data is shared only with our authorised staff who are directly involved in processing your loan.

4. Data Security

All data is stored securely on protected systems. Uploaded documents are stored in private, non-public server directories and can only be accessed by authorised staff or the document owner through the portal.

5. Cookies & Portal Usage

Our customer portal uses session cookies to keep you logged in. No tracking or advertising cookies are used. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though this will affect portal functionality.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us directly. We will respond to all requests within a reasonable time period.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related queries, please contact us at {{ site.email }} or visit our office.
